# The team

Each hire is a skill you invoke. Roles, never people.

## On a cadence

| Role | Skill | Cadence | Reads | Writes | Holds you to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | `/growth-setup` | once, then each quarter | the folder you opened, your answers | mission, OKRs, derailers, first experiment, `PROFILE.md` | a role profile and a first experiment before anything else runs |
| Chief of staff | `/standup` (`morning-standup`) | each day in your cadence | the previous standup, `experiments/`, and whatever calendar, task or analytics tool you have connected (none by default; see `STACK.md`) | `retros/standups/<date>.md` | one thing that must be true by tonight, and a verdict on yesterday's |
| Head of growth | `/weekly-retro` | your retro day (Friday by default) | the quarter's OKRs, this week's standups, `experiments/` | `retros/weekly/<date>.md` | every experiment past its window is measured or killed before the week is scored |
| The board | `/monthly-retro` | first week of the month | the mission, the quarter, the month's weeklies, the compounding ledger (`retros/compounding-ledger.md`: what shipped and still pays off with your hands off it) | `retros/monthly/<month>.md`, the ledger | the year line, not the task list |
| Critic bench | `/multi-agent-refine` | before anything ships | the draft | the revised draft | a clean round from three or four sceptical critics |
| Product lead | `/prd` | before anything is built | the goal, the experiment log, the codebase | a milestone-checkbox PRD | decisions captured before a line of code |

## On call

| Role | Skill | Does | Reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO analyst | `/seo-audit` | finds the problem on a page and stages the experiment that tests the fix | the live site, keyword data |
| Editor | `/tone-of-voice`, `/humanizer` | locks your voice from evidence, then strips AI tells from anything before it ships | your own writing, the draft |
| Ghostwriter | `/linkedin-post`, `/copywriting-hooks` | drafts, runs the pre-publish gate, fixes silently, files the gate report | the voice file, the brief |
| Video researcher | `/youtube-package`, `/vidiq-competitive` | titles from real keyword data, qualify or kill a topic before you film | the transcript, vidIQ |
| Skill smith | `/skill-iterator` | how you add your own hire: test cases first, grade, promote | the skill under test |

Put the cadence roles on a schedule (Claude Code routines, or cron on your own machine) if you want them to run without you. Each skill says how.

If you're in a team: the cadence roles run per person; the critic bench and the on-call roles are shared.
