# Compounding ledger

The list of things that keep producing value with your hands off them. It answers the only question a solo operator cannot answer from a busy calendar: what did I build that is still working?

**Admission test (strict).** A row is admitted only when all three hold in the month of admission:

1. It produced something: a subscriber, a reply, a sale, a published thing, an email that sent itself.
2. It did so with your hands off it. If you had to touch it that month, it is a task, not an asset.
3. The evidence is named in the row: what, when, where you read it. "Seems to be working" does not admit.

Builds and activities do not qualify until they have produced something. A rebuilt sequence is a build; three replies to it are evidence. Reassess anything refused next month.

**Rules.** Never delete a row. A dead asset gets a strikethrough and the date it stopped. Keep the live list short; past about fifteen rows the test is being applied too loosely.

Rows marked `(example)` are documentation shipped with the plugin, not your assets. The monthly retro ignores them, and you may delete them once a real row exists.
