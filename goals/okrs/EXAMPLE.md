# OKRs - 2026 Q3 (example)

**Mission line this quarter serves:** 2,000 people on the email list and £5,000/month from the digital product by 31 Dec.
**Scored:** every Friday in the weekly retro, each KR 0–100%.

## Objectives

| Id | Objective | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Grow the email list from 1,000 to 2,000 | Building instead of publishing: a product task as the one thing three days running |
| O2 | Lift the pricing page checkout rate from 3.2% | Polishing past good-enough: the variant sitting in `planned` while the copy gets "ready" |
| O3 | Publish weekly without a gap | Inbox as the day's plan: standup written after 10:00 |

## Key results

| KR | Objective | Type | Baseline | Target | Experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KR1.1 | O1 | behaviour | one lead magnet, unpromoted | mention it in every post | n/a |
| KR1.2 | O1 | outcome | 1,000 subscribers | 1,250 by quarter end | `../../experiments/welcome-sequence-v2.md` |
| KR2.1 | O2 | behaviour | no test running | one pricing test live at all times, with a power check | n/a |
| KR2.2 | O2 | outcome | 3.2% checkout rate | measured winner, whatever the number | `../../experiments/pricing-page-headline.md` |
| KR3.1 | O3 | behaviour | published 7 of the last 13 weeks | 13 of 13 | n/a |

## Dropped this quarter

- The second product. The first one has not hit £5,000/month.
- Redesigning the site. The pricing page test is the only page work that counts.
- Paid ads. Not until the welcome sequence is measured.

## Which objective matters most

O3. Without the weekly post, O1 has no traffic and O2 has nothing to test on.
