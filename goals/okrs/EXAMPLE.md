# OKRs - 2026 Q3 (example)

**Mission line this quarter serves:** £1M ARR by 31 Dec.
**Scored:** every Friday in the weekly retro, each KR 0–100%.

## Objectives

| Id | Objective | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Lift lead → customer conversion from 10% to 20% | Polishing past good-enough: the variant sitting in `planned` while the copy gets "ready" |
| O2 | Grow the email list from 1,000 to 2,000 | Building instead of publishing: a product task as the one thing three days running |

## Key results

| KR | Objective | Type | Baseline | Target | Experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KR1.1 | O1 | behaviour | no test running | one conversion test live at all times, with a power check | n/a |
| KR1.2 | O1 | outcome | 10% lead → customer conversion | 20% | `../../experiments/EXAMPLE-live-stale.md` |
| KR2.1 | O2 | behaviour | published 7 of the last 13 weeks | publish every Tuesday, 13 of 13 | n/a |
| KR2.2 | O2 | outcome | 1,000 on the list | 1,250 by quarter end | `../../experiments/EXAMPLE-killed.md` |

## Dropped this quarter

- The second product. The first one is not converting at 20% yet.
- Redesigning the site. The pricing page test is the only page work that counts.
- Paid ads. Not until the welcome sequence is measured.

## Which objective matters most

O1. Doubling conversion doubles what every lead is worth, including the ones the list already sends. A bigger list at 10% is the same business with more email.
